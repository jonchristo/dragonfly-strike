    ROTORWASHBLUE VS RED

    Two armies fight for five objectives on an open test range. Riflemen, machine-gun teams, anti-tank crews, mortars, tanks and artillery on both sides. You fly the Blue gunship: clear the way with the 30 mm gun, rockets and guided missiles, call in air assaults, artillery and air strikes, and keep the balance tipped Blue.

    Winning: each side has a reserve pool. Every soldier or vehicle lost costs its side, and holding more objectives than the enemy bleeds their pool. Empty Red's pool to win. Watch for SAMs: when the MISSILE warning flashes, pop flares and break hard. Land on the FARP pad to rearm, refuel and drop off freed POWs.
