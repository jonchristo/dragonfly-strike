# Arcane Front

A Minecraft-style voxel war game in the browser, set in a country that looks a lot like Wisconsin. You pilot the Arcane Vanguard's experimental flight mech against three ridiculous militias (the Bratwurst Brigade, the Supper Club Syndicate and the Cheddar Cartel) who have dug into twelve towns across the state.

## The world

A 384 × 384 block map shaped like Wisconsin: Lake Michigan with Door County and Green Bay, Lake Superior with the Apostle Islands, the Mississippi and St. Croix on the west, the Wisconsin, Chippewa, Fox and Rock rivers, Lake Winnebago, Madison's lakes and dozens of Northwoods lakes. Driftless hills in the southwest, Rib Mountain, the Baraboo Hills, the Central Sands pine country, and October colors in the north.

Milwaukee's towers on the lakefront, the capitol dome in Madison, a stadium in Green Bay, about 25 smaller towns, red-barn dairy farms with blue silos, corn, soybean and wheat fields, and interstates connecting it all. Cows, deer, black bears, geese, civilians and highway traffic go about their day. They get launched by explosions and bounce back ("Ope! I'm okay!").

## Combat

- Flight mech: hold to climb, hover assist holds altitude like a helicopter, turn it off for manual thrust; boost, rotor wash, superhero landings
- Guns: battle rifle, auto shotgun, .50 cal sniper (4×/8×/12× scope, bullet drop with holdover marks, hold breath, penetration, slow-motion long headshots), rocket launcher, minigun
- Plasma saber: swing (lunges at nearby targets, cuts through walls and trees), block and deflect bullets and rockets back, throw it like a boomerang
- Orbital uplink: kinetic rod from space, a steerable orbital laser, and a dropship that drops six troopers and strafes with its door gun
- Command tablet: airstrike, artillery, cruise missile, supply drop, sentry turret, fireteam, forward operating base
- Fortify mode: place sandbags, concrete and steel for 2 supply per block
- Ragdolls, flying limbs and hats, ketchup splats, death quips, kill streaks

Open `index.html` in Chrome or Edge. Keyboard and mouse, or an Xbox controller (press any button to connect).
