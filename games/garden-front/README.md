# Garden Front

**Game 2 · Air support over a live ground war**

The sequel to Dragonfly Strike. Ants and ladybugs fight a real ground war beneath you across five control points, with a tug-of-war battle meter. Spend command points on battalion drops, acorn artillery and bumblebee bombers while you fly support.

**Play:** open `index.html` in Chrome or Edge.

**Xbox controller:** left stick fly · right stick aim · LB/RB spin · RT or A gun · X bombs · B missile · D-pad ◀▶ plus Y to choose and call in support · Menu pause. Keyboard and touch also work.
