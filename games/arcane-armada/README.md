# Arcane Armada

**Game 6 · The mothership fork of Arcane Overdrive**

Arcane Overdrive with a way to win. Two motherships hang over opposite ends of Wisconsin: your **Vanguard Ark** in the west and the Coalition dreadnought everyone calls **The Big Cheese**, parked over Lake Michigan. The Brigade, Syndicate and Cartel now fight as one Coalition. Take ground, strip the enemy shield, then destroy the mothership.

The previous version is still playable unchanged as Game 5, [Arcane Overdrive](../arcane-overdrive/).

## How a war is won

- **Territory is the key.** There are 12 strongholds. You start with 4 on the western front and the Coalition holds 8.
- **Hold 9 of 12 (70%)** and the Big Cheese's shield collapses. The Ark sails across the map to duel it with its main cannons and sends strike gunships at its reactors.
- **Destroy all 4 reactor cores** under the Big Cheese's hull to bring it down. Eight point-defense guns and interceptor gunships protect it. Each core you destroy pays 1,500 supply.
- **Lose 70% of Wisconsin** and the Ark's shield drops instead. The Big Cheese advances on it. Retake strongholds to restore the shield before the Ark's cores are gone. If the Ark falls, the war is lost.
- Lose ground and the shield comes back up. Destroyed cores stay destroyed.

## Mothership abilities (both sides)

- **Artillery:** ten heavy shells on a stronghold, telegraphed by a marker. The Big Cheese aims at whichever of your strongholds you are standing in.
- **Heavy machine guns:** eight point-defense turrets fire at anything that comes close.
- **Troop drops:** gunships launch from the hangar with six troopers to take or reinforce a stronghold.
- **Lance:** a beam carves a trench across a target. The Big Cheese will lock on to you if you fly within range, so dash.
- **Strike craft and interceptors:** gunships that hunt the enemy mothership's reactors, or defend their own.
- **Your Ark:** fly within 55 m of it to repair armor and uplink. The command tablet adds **Ark Barrage** (500) and **Ark Lance** (750) so you can call the Ark's guns yourself.

## A zoned, readable Wisconsin

- **Cities have real zoning.** A downtown of towers ringed by a commercial district (offices, apartments, storefronts, parking), then city neighborhoods of rowhouses, then suburbs of houses with lawns, driveways, a school, a strip mall and a water tower. Each metro has an industrial park of warehouses, smokestacks and tank farms.
- **Small towns** have a brick Main Street with a church and a water tower, with houses around it. No skyscrapers outside downtowns.
- **Farm country** has straight county roads, with farmhouses along them and gravel driveways. Woods and the Northwoods stay roadless forest.
- **The HUD tells you where you are:** Downtown · Milwaukee, Suburbs · Madison, Industrial park · Green Bay, Farmland · near Tomah, Northwoods.

## Still here from Overdrive

Meteor Drop, shoulder charge, dash anywhere, collapse physics, rampage meter, war-mech stagger fights, shock troopers, persistent gunships, enemy assault waves with AA troops, fortified FOBs and the techno soundtrack.
