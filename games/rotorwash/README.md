# Rotorwash

**Game 3 · Attack-helicopter combined-arms battle**

A red-vs-blue battlefield sim. Fly an Apache over infantry, machine-gun nests, anti-tank teams, mortars, tanks, anti-air and artillery. Ragdoll physics, flying debris, flares against SAMs, and air assault, artillery and air strike call-ins.

**Play:** open `index.html` in Chrome or Edge.

**Xbox controller:** left stick fly · right stick aim · LB/RB spin · LT hold heading · RT or A gun · X rockets · B missile · D-pad ◀▶ plus Y to call in support · Menu pause. Keyboard and touch also work.
