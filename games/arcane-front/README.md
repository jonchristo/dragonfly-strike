# Arcane Front

**Game 4 · Mech combat across a voxel Wisconsin**

A mech combat game in a Minecraft-style voxel world shaped like Wisconsin. You pilot the Arcane Vanguard's MK-7 assault mech against three ridiculous militias (the Bratwurst Brigade, the Supper Club Syndicate and the Cheddar Cartel) and the war mechs they drop in without warning. Retake all twelve strongholds to win.

## The mech

- **Walk mode:** fast stomping run that smashes through houses, barns, trees, fences and cars in your way. Jump and hold for jump jets, dash sideways, and slam down for a shockwave landing.
- **Hover mode:** jets lift you off; the suit holds altitude like a helicopter.
- **Pulse laser machine gun** (shoulder cannon) and **homing missile salvo** (shoulder pod, locks up to six targets) are always available.
- **Arm weapons:** plasma saber (swing, block and deflect, throw), Rail Lance (piercing laser sniper with 4×/8×/12× scope), Beam Cannon (continuous laser that cuts through terrain).
- **Orbital uplink:** kinetic rod, steerable orbital laser, dropship with six troopers.
- Lasers share one heat gauge; overheat and you vent for two seconds.

## The world

Lake Michigan, Door County, Green Bay, Lake Superior, the Mississippi and Wisconsin rivers, Lake Winnebago, Madison's capitol between its lakes, Milwaukee's lakefront towers, about 25 more towns, dairy farms, highways with traffic, and cows, deer, bears, geese and civilians who get launched and bounce back.

Open `index.html` in Chrome or Edge. Keyboard and mouse, or an Xbox controller (press any button to connect).
